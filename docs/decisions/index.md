# Decision records

BacSelect records important scientific and technical design choices separately
from the implementation.

A decision record explains:

1. the problem being addressed;
2. the current decision;
3. why that choice was made;
4. alternatives considered;
5. consequences and limitations;
6. whether the decision is final or still validation-dependent.
