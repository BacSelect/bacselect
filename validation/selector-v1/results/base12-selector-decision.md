# Base-12 OPS versus SR decision record

## Status

UNRESOLVED

Identity blinding remains in force.

No BacSelect selector has been chosen by this comparison.

## Primary comparison

The pre-specified primary metric is the species-balanced weighted 95th
percentile nearest-panel Euclidean distance.

| N | Lower primary metric |
|---:|:---|
| 10 | OPS |
| 20 | SR |
| 50 | SR |
| 100 | SR |
| 200 | SR |
| 500 | SR |

The primary curves are therefore not uniformly ordered.

Under the prospective decision rule, this does not produce an automatic
selector winner.

## Pre-specified secondary metrics

The secondary metrics also remain mixed.

At N=10, OPS has lower weighted mean, weighted median and weighted p95
distances and lower values for most species-level summaries, while SR has
lower extreme maximum distances.

At N=20, SR has lower weighted mean, weighted median and weighted p95
distances and lower values for several species-level summaries, while OPS
has lower maximum-related values.

At N=50, the secondary metrics are mixed: OPS has lower mean- and
median-related values, while SR has lower tail and extreme-distance values.

At N=100 and N=200, most secondary coverage summaries are lower for SR,
although some extreme-distance summaries remain lower for OPS.

At N=500, every pre-specified secondary coverage metric is lower for SR.

## Decision

The base-12 comparison remains unresolved.

SR shows broader structural-coverage advantages from N=20 onward, but the
pre-specified primary curves cross and the secondary metrics are not uniformly
ordered.

No new metric or post-hoc decision rule is introduced.

Genome and species identities remain blinded.

The remaining pre-specified validation analyses will proceed before any
selector decision or unblinding.
